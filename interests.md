# Research & Professional Interests

My interests lie at the intersection of **wireless communications, digital signal processing, and machine learning**. I am particularly interested in developing signal processing and learning-based techniques that improve the reliability, efficiency, and adaptability of modern wireless communication systems.

## Wireless Communications & Physical Layer Design

I am interested in physical-layer algorithms for next-generation wireless systems, with a focus on:

- **MIMO and OFDM Systems:** Physical-layer modem design, channel estimation, synchronization, and signal detection.
- **Time-Varying Wireless Channels:** Channel tracking and adaptive signal processing in multipath and high-mobility environments.
- **OTFS Modulation:** Signal processing and machine learning techniques for communication in high-mobility, delay-Doppler environments.
- **Satellite Communications:** MIMO-based physical-layer design and implementation considerations for satellite communication systems.

## AI/ML for Wireless Communications

I am interested in applying machine learning and deep learning to challenging wireless signal processing problems, particularly where conventional approaches face limitations.

My work and interests include:

- **Deep Learning for PHY:** Learning-based approaches to channel estimation, carrier frequency offset estimation, and automatic modulation classification.
- **RF Fingerprinting:** Identifying wireless transmitters through hardware-induced signal characteristics.
- **Neural Architectures for Signal Processing:** Exploring operational neural networks, convolutional architectures, attention mechanisms, and feature-learning techniques for wireless applications.
- **Learning Under Channel Impairments:** Developing robust models for multipath propagation, Doppler effects, noise, and nonlinear transmitter characteristics.

## Digital Signal Processing & Hardware-Aware Design

I am also interested in translating signal processing algorithms into practical implementations, considering the constraints imposed by real-time systems and hardware platforms.

Areas of interest include:

- Fixed-point signal processing and quantization effects.
- Algorithm complexity, computational efficiency, and resource optimization.
- Synchronization and channel tracking for practical communication receivers.
- MATLAB-based algorithm development and simulation, with an interest in hardware-oriented implementation using C and RTL workflows.

## Multimodal Sensing & Intelligent Wireless Systems

My research internship at the Wireless Communication Lab, UNSW, introduced me to multimodal sensor fusion for wireless beam prediction. This motivated my interest in combining information from different sensing modalities to improve communication-system decisions.

I am particularly interested in:

- Vision- and radar-assisted wireless communication.
- Multimodal sensor fusion for beam prediction and beam management.
- Machine learning approaches for exploiting contextual information in wireless systems.
- Efficient use of sensing information to improve communication reliability and reduce overhead.

## Professional Interests

I enjoy working on problems that connect theoretical signal processing with practical wireless-system development. I am particularly interested in research and engineering roles involving:

- Wireless PHY algorithm development.
- Digital signal processing and communication receiver design.
- AI/ML applications in wireless communications.
- Satellite communications and high-mobility wireless systems.
- Signal processing algorithm optimization and hardware implementation.

**Technical Tools:** Python, C, MATLAB, PyTorch, and TensorFlow.
